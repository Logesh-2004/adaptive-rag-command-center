AI System Design Principles

Modern AI systems require more than just model inference.

Key Principles:
1. Modularity: Separate ingestion, retrieval, generation, and evaluation.
2. Observability: Track latency, errors, and outputs.
3. Reliability: Prevent hallucinations using grounding checks.
4. Scalability: Handle increasing data and queries efficiently.

Production AI systems often include:
- Feedback loops for continuous improvement.
- Monitoring dashboards.
- Data versioning.

Failure Modes:
- Incorrect retrieval
- Overconfidence in LLM outputs
- Poor evaluation metrics

A robust AI system must detect and handle its own failures.
