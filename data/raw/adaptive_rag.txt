Adaptive RAG (Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is an advanced form of RAG that dynamically adjusts retrieval and generation strategies based on query complexity, confidence, or evaluation signals.

Unlike traditional RAG systems that always retrieve the same way, Adaptive RAG can:
- Decide when retrieval is necessary
- Dynamically rerank retrieved documents
- Use evaluators to assess grounding and faithfulness
- Trigger corrective retrieval if confidence is low
- Route queries through different pipelines

Key Components:
- Query Router
- Retriever
- Reranker
- Generator (LLM)
- Evaluator
- Feedback Loop

Adaptive RAG improves answer quality, reduces hallucinations, and enables more reliable AI systems.
